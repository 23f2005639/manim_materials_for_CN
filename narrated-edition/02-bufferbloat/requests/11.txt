[warmly] That four hundred milliseconds is only the wait at this doorway. Signals also need time to travel. Devices need time to examine data, and putting a packet onto a link takes time too. A reply must make a journey back. Those other delays have not vanished; we simply left them out while measuring this one queue. The important point is visible in the bar: a large waiting line can add a big chunk of delay to an otherwise working connection.
