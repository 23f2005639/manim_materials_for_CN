[curious] Our waiting room is not endless. Fill every space, then bring in another packet. There may be nowhere to put it, so the device discards it. That is packet loss. Waiting and being discarded are different problems: one packet arrives late, another does not arrive through this attempt. A larger room may delay the moment it fills, while also allowing longer waits. Real devices can manage their queues more carefully. Our drawing explains why simply adding storage is not enough.
