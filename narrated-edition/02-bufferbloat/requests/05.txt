[warmly] Imagine a large download arriving as lots of blue packets. [short pause] For a while, they reach our doorway faster than it can send them onward. One leaves, but more join. A line builds up. The exit keeps working, which looks great on a download counter. Yet look inside the waiting room. That pile is growing. We have changed the amount of waiting without making the doorway any narrower. This is the clue that a speed test can miss.
