[warmly] Compare these two doors. [short pause] Both send data at the same rate. The top has a long standing queue; the bottom has a shorter one. Watch the blue packets already ahead leave first in both rows. Green gets its turn much earlier in the shorter line. It has not jumped the queue, and the door has not become faster. This is what better queue management aims to improve: keep the connection useful while avoiding a big pile of data that waits unnecessarily.
