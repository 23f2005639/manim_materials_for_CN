[warmly] Now the name makes sense. Bufferbloat is excessive delay caused by too much data sitting in buffers when a connection is busy. Throughput means how much data gets through; delay means how long something takes. A connection can have good throughput and still respond slowly. Bufferbloat is one possible cause of lag, not the answer to every connection problem. Next time a download looks fast but a conversation feels late, remember our green box.
