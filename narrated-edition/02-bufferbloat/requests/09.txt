[warmly] Before using real numbers, we need one small unit check. [short pause] A byte is a group of eight bits. Files are often measured in bytes, while connection rates are often given in bits per second. Our waiting data is one megabyte. Here, mega means one million, so that is one million bytes. Expand each byte into eight bits and we get eight million bits. Now both our waiting amount and our outgoing rate can use the same unit. The division will make sense.
