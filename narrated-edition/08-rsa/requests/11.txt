[warmly] The interesting question is why seven returns. For numbers such as seven, which share no factor with fifty-five except one, these powers have a repeating structure linked to our count of forty. Encrypting with power three and decrypting with power twenty-seven combines into power eighty-one. That is one plus two times forty. The matching construction brings us back to the original value. The circle illustrates repetition; it is not a claim that every message takes exactly forty steps to repeat.
