[warmly] Step back from the arithmetic. [short pause] With a shared-secret method, the people communicating must have a suitable secret in common. With this public-key arrangement, the receiving person can publish the encrypting instructions and keep the recovery instructions private. The private key is related to the public key, but should be impractical to derive from it. That last condition matters. Publishing an operation is useful only if seeing how to perform it does not make recovering the private operation easy.
