[warmly] Choose five and eleven. Both are prime numbers: whole numbers greater than one that divide evenly only by one and themselves. Primes act like building blocks for multiplication. Multiply our two choices and we get fifty-five. That becomes the number we will use when keeping remainders. We call it n, simply a short label. Our public instructions will include fifty-five. The two factors, five and eleven, help us build the private instructions. These tiny numbers are for seeing the idea.
