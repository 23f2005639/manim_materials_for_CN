[warmly] The private rule asks us to use a much bigger power. Must we write an enormous number first? [short pause] [warmly] Happily, no. We can keep remainders while calculating. Start with thirteen. Square it, meaning multiply it by itself: one hundred and sixty-nine leaves four after division by fifty-five. Square that four to get sixteen. Square sixteen and keep its remainder, thirty-six. Each step keeps the same useful remainder information while preventing the working numbers from growing without limit.
