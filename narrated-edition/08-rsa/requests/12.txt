[curious] What if we replace twenty-seven with a guess? [short pause] [warmly] Use five instead. The same encrypted number thirteen now produces forty-three, not seven. The right side uses twenty-seven and recovers seven. So the private rule is not simply any large number or any secret number. It must fit the public construction. This small experiment does not prove security. It shows what correctness requires: the intended pair must undo the message transformation, while an arbitrary guess generally does not.
