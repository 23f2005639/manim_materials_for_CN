[warmly] We wanted to send a private message without first handing the sender our opening secret. The lock picture suggested separate public and private jobs. Our tiny RSA example made those jobs concrete: seven became thirteen using the public pair fifty-five and three; thirteen returned to seven using the private power twenty-seven. Remainders kept the arithmetic inside a limited set of values. The carefully chosen relationship made recovery work. That is the idea beneath RSA: shared public instructions, paired with private recovery.
