[warmly] Picture your friend handing out open padlocks while keeping the opening key. Anyone can put a message in a box and snap a lock shut. Only your friend can open it. RSA uses a mathematical version of two different jobs: a public rule for encrypting and a private rule for recovering the message. Computers represent messages as numbers, so our tiny demonstration will protect the number seven. The padlock is an analogy; the real work will be arithmetic.
