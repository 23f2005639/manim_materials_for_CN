[curious] There is another reason not to use our little calculation directly as real encryption. Encrypt seven twice with this bare rule and thirteen appears twice. That visible repetition can leak information. Practical encryption needs a complete, carefully designed encoding scheme that adds the right structure and randomness before the RSA operation. We are looking inside one mathematical mechanism, not building a safe messaging app. The small numbers let us understand the moving parts without pretending that understanding alone makes them secure.
