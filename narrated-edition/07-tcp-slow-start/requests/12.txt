[curious] Can we keep doubling forever? [short pause] [warmly] Watch the narrow link in the middle. A link is one connection along the route. This one can pass data more slowly than the sender now brings it. Pieces start waiting, just like people arriving faster than a door lets them through. This limiting connection is called a bottleneck. More data in the waiting line does not make that connection faster. Eventually the sender needs to change how it grows, rather than doubling without a boundary.
