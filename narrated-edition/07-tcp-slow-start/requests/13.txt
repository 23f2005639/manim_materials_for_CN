[warmly] TCP uses a boundary called the slow-start threshold. The short label on the diagram is ssthresh. Below that boundary, slow-start growth can continue. At the boundary, the sender moves to another growth phase. In our picture we chose thirty-two so you can see where the steep curve stops. This is a teaching value, not a number shared by every connection. Loss and other feedback can also change the behavior. Today we're following this opening growth idea, not every TCP rule.
