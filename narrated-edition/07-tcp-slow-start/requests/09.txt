[warmly] We can write what the picture already showed. [short pause] Begin with four, then multiply by two once for every completed round. After k rounds, the allowance is four times two to the power k. That power just means repeated multiplication: after two rounds, four times two times two equals sixteen. At zero rounds we still have four. You don't need the formula to discover the pattern; it is a short way to describe the doubling we just watched.
