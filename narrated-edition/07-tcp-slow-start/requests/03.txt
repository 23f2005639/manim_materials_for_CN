[curious] Let's begin with an allowance of four pieces. The four empty boxes are spaces for data we have sent but haven't yet had confirmed. This allowance is called the congestion window, often written cwnd. Here, window means a limit, not something on your screen. We chose four for this demonstration; real starting values can differ. Also assume the receiver has plenty of room. That lets us focus on how the sender grows its allowance using feedback from the journey.
