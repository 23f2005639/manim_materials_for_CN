[curious] Look at these addresses: they all start with 192.168.1, while the last number changes. Those first three numbers use twenty-four bits. If we keep those bits fixed, we get one group with eight positions left to change. We write the group as 192.168.1.0 slash twenty-four. The slash tells us how many beginning bits stay fixed. That shared beginning is called a prefix. It is simply the part every address in this group has in common.
