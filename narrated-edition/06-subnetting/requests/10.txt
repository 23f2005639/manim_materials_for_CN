[warmly] Try the address ending in seventy. [short pause] In bits, seventy is zero-one-zero-zero-zero-one-one-zero. Place our mask underneath it. Keep an address bit where the mask has a one; replace it with zero where the mask has a zero. This operation is called AND. The answer is zero-one followed by six zeros: sixty-four. So seventy belongs to the group starting at sixty-four. The mask has erased the individual device's part and left the group's starting address behind.
