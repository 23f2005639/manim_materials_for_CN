[warmly] One free bit has two choices: zero or one. Two free bits have four: zero-zero, zero-one, one-zero, one-one. Every extra free bit doubles the choices. Keep doubling until eight bits give two hundred and fifty-six possibilities. We write that as two to the power of eight. The final number can therefore run from zero through two hundred and fifty-five. Zero counts as a possibility too. Now we know the size of the group we are about to divide.
