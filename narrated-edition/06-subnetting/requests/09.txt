[warmly] A computer needs a way to mark which bits belong to the shared beginning. [short pause] We use a mask, like a stencil: ones mark the shared positions, zeros mark the changing positions. Our first twenty-four bits are shared, plus two more in the final group of eight. That last pattern is one-one followed by six zeros. The first two positions are worth one hundred and twenty-eight and sixty-four. Add them to get one hundred and ninety-two. Our mask is 255.255.255.192.
