[warmly] A byte contains eight bits. [short pause] Our example packet has fifteen hundred bytes, so multiply by eight to get twelve thousand bits. The link sends ten million bits each second. Divide twelve thousand by ten million and we get zero point zero zero one two seconds: one point two milliseconds. A millisecond is one thousandth of a second. The shorthand is L divided by R: length in bits divided by rate. This measures putting data onto the link; signal travel time is extra.
