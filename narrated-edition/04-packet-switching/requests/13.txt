[warmly] There is a limit. If packets arrive at a device faster than its outgoing link can send them, they form a waiting line, called a queue. Green can still get stuck behind blue. Splitting the picture does not create an endlessly fast connection or a perfectly fair order. If the waiting space fills, a packet may even be discarded. So our claim is modest and useful: packet boundaries give the network manageable turns to share, while actual capacity and queue choices still matter.
