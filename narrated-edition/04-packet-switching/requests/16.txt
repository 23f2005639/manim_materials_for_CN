[warmly] We began with one picture on a screen. Breaking its data into packets gave us shorter turns on a shared link. Those pieces also let several links work at once, just like the stations in our parcel line. We still pay for headers, we can still wait in queues, and every link has a limit. But now a big picture and a tiny message can use the same network in manageable pieces. The Internet's useful trick starts with something simple: divide the work.
