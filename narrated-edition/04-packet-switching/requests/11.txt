[warmly] Now the formula is just a short version of our counting. Call the number of links H and the number of packets N. The first packet needs H turns. The remaining N minus one packets each add a turn. Together, that is H plus N minus one. Multiply by the time for one turn, L divided by R. That gives our total. It works for this clean model with equal packets and equal-rate links, without extra travel, processing, or traffic delays.
