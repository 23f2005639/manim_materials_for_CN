[curious] Why not make every piece tiny? [short pause] [warmly] Short turns sound wonderful. But remember the handling information added to each packet. That information uses space too. Blue is useful picture data; yellow is the extra label, called a header. Divide the same blue data into more pieces and count the yellow labels. There are more of them. Devices also have more individual packets to handle. Smaller pieces can help sharing, but they are not free. We must balance shorter turns against this extra work.
