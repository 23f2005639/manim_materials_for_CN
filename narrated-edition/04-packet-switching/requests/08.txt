[warmly] Packet one has reached the first switch. It is now being sent on link two. Look back at link one: it is free. Why leave it doing nothing? [short pause] [warmly] Send packet two there. A moment later, packet one uses link three, packet two uses link two, and packet three can use link one. Different parts of the path work at once. This is called a pipeline, like several parcels moving through different stations. Each parcel still visits every station in the proper order.
