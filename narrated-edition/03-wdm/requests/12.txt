[curious] There is one small trap in that impressive number. File sizes often use bytes, while link rates often use bits. A byte is a group of eight bits. Watch eight little boxes become one group. To turn four hundred gigabits per second into gigabytes per second, divide by eight. The answer is fifty. That is a raw capacity conversion, not a promise that a download reaches fifty gigabytes every second. The rest of the system also takes time and capacity.
