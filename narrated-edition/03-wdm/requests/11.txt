[warmly] Now the numbers have a picture to attach to. A bit rate tells us how many bits a channel carries each second. Suppose each of our four channels carries one hundred billion bits per second. We shorten that to one hundred gigabits per second, written Gb/s. Add the channels one at a time: one hundred, two hundred, three hundred, four hundred. Four equal channels provide four hundred gigabits per second of combined raw capacity in this example.
