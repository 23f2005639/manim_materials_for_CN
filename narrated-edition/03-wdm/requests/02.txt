[warmly] How can light stand for a photo? [short pause] [warmly] Start much smaller: a yes or a no. We can agree that a bright pulse means one and a dark time means zero. Each choice is called a bit. Put many bits together, and their pattern can represent text, sound, or a picture. Our blinking light is a simple teaching example. Real equipment can use more detailed patterns, but the basic job stays the same: carry information using changes.
