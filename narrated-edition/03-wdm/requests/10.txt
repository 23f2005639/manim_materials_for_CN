[warmly] Let us follow only the green channel. [short pause] Dim the others so your eye can stay with one bit pattern. Green enters, travels alongside the other light, and reaches the green output. Its receiver reads those changes. It does not need the blue message to stop talking first. This is the central trick: the shared fiber carries several distinguishable channels at once. Our labels track their identities; the actual separation is performed by optical equipment designed for their wavelengths.
