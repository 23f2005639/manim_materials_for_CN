[warmly] If four channels help, why not squeeze in endlessly more? [short pause] [warmly] Look at the spaces between the channels. The equipment has a usable range, and the signals need enough room to remain distinguishable. Add too many into the same space and separation becomes harder. Noise and other physical effects matter too. Engineers must match the fiber, light sources, amplifiers, and receivers. More channels can increase capacity, but the drawing cannot create unlimited room that real equipment does not have.
