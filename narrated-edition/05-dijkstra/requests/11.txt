[warmly] Why trust the smallest unfinished score? [short pause] [warmly] Every connection in our picture adds a positive cost. Suppose the cheapest unfinished dot costs eight. A route that first goes through an unfinished dot costing ten cannot come back and beat eight; adding more positive prices only makes ten larger. That is the key idea behind the recipe. Zero-cost connections also work. Negative prices would break this reasoning, so this version of the algorithm requires costs that never go below zero.
