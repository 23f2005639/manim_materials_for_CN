[warmly] Think about calling a friend. You remember their name; your phone uses a number. Websites have a similar split. We type a name, while the network uses a number called an IP address to help deliver messages. Watch the name turn into an address. DNS, short for Domain Name System, helps make that lookup. Our pretend website is photos.orbit.example. The address is made up for teaching too. You don't need to memorize either one.
