# References — TCP vs UDP: The Cost of Reliability

Sources checked while writing the teaching script; narration is original.

- [RFC 9293 — Transmission Control Protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9293) — Byte-stream service, sequence numbers, SYN/FIN sequence space, three-way handshake, cumulative acknowledgments and receive windows.
- [RFC 768 — User Datagram Protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc768) — UDP datagram service does not guarantee delivery or duplicate protection.
- [RFC 5681 — TCP Congestion Control](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5681) — Congestion window and receiver window are distinct constraints on sending.
- [RFC 9000 — QUIC: A UDP-Based Multiplexed and Secure Transport](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9000) — QUIC demonstrates reliable transport capabilities layered over UDP datagrams.
